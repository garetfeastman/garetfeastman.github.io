AUX Together privacy and terms

AUX Together is a personal listen-along page run by Garet for friends. It isn't a business and doesn't sell anything.

What sign-in shares

When you sign in with Google, the page gets your name, email address and profile picture. Your name and picture appear next to your chat messages. Your email is shown only to the room owner, so they can pick who can request songs.

Where it goes

Chat messages, your name, picture and email travel through a public message service (HiveMQ's public broker) so everyone in the room sees them. Anyone who knows a room's code could read that room's messages, so don't post anything private. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Music

Playback uses YouTube's embedded player, which follows YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

Removing your info

Email garetfeastman@gmail.com and your messages and room listing will be cleared.

Terms

Use it to hang out and listen together. Be decent in chat. The owner can remove anyone's song-request permission at any time. Provided as-is, with no guarantees.
